Privacy Policy

Salat: Prayer Garden · Last updated 23 November 2026

The short version

Your prayers, score and location stay on your iPhone. We never see them and never sell them. Salat is free and shows a few ads, served by Google AdMob; ad partners may receive your device's advertising identifier and basic device information to show and measure ads. You choose whether ads may be personalised.

What stays on your device

Your location. Used only to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is processed on your iPhone and is never sent to us, to Google or to anyone else. To show your city's name, iOS may look up your coordinates with Apple's geocoding service, under Apple's privacy policy.

Your prayer log, score, streak, milestones, rewards and settings. Stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage, which its widgets can read. Deleting the app deletes this data. Nothing about your prayers is ever shared with advertisers.

Compass heading. Read from your iPhone's sensors while the Qibla screen is open, and never stored.

Notifications. Prayer reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No notification server is involved.

Photos. Only if you tap "Save image" on a verse card, Salat adds that one image to your photo library. It never reads your photos.

Ads

Salat shows a small number of ads to stay free: one short ad on some app openings (at most once a day, never when you open a prayer notification or a widget), one before starting your garden over, an occasional ad after saving or sharing a verse card, a single sponsored card at the bottom of the Journey screen, and short ads you choose to watch to unlock extras. Apart from ads you choose to watch and the one before starting over, there are none in your first three days. There are never ads on the prayer list or the Qibla screen.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when iOS asks), your IP address, device type, language and app interactions with ads. Ads are limited to content suitable for all audiences.

Your choices. Apple's tracking prompt lets you refuse tracking; ads then are not personalised. In regions where the law requires it, a consent form lets you choose how your data is used for ads, and you can change that choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Purchases

Where offered, "Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether you own the purchase.

What we collect

Nothing. Salat has no server of its own and no account. The only data leaving your iPhone is what the ad service needs to show ads, described above.

Children

Salat is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: hello@sleiman.info
