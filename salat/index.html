Support

Prayer times look wrong

Open Settings → Prayer times and pick the calculation method your local masjid uses. You can also shift any prayer by a few minutes under Adjust minutes. Make sure location access is allowed so Salat knows where you are.

I prayed but forgot to log it

Tap any day in the week strip on the Today screen. You can mark prayers from the last 7 days.

I don't get reminders

Check that notifications for Salat are allowed in the iPhone Settings app, and that reminders are on in Salat → Settings. Open the app at least once a week so it can schedule the next week of prayer times.

Can I turn off the adhan sound?

Yes. Salat → Settings → Notifications → Call at adhan time.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep Salat free. There are never any on the prayer list or the Qibla screen, and apart from ads you choose to watch and the one before starting over, none in your first three days. You can change your ad privacy choices in Settings where your region requires it.
